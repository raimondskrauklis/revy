## Cross-cutting

| Severity | Area | Finding |
|:---|:---|:---|
| critical | codebase | Mid-LLM abort / SoftTimeLimit / permanent `_mark_failed` rolls back or never writes retrieve/review observability. P0 “checkpoint commit” and `LlmCallRecorder` must define **commit graph**: (1) checkpoint txn after retrieve, (2) attempt-start txn before HTTP, (3) attempt complete/fail update, (4) worker final commit, (5) `_mark_failed` enrichment of `failure_stage`/`failure_class` when main txn never committed. |
| critical | codebase | `httpx.TimeoutException` is retryable (up to 3). Without early commits, attempt rows + `context_stats` vanish on each retry. Edge-case table mentions timeout attempt rows but not **cross-retry durability**. |

| high | general plan | Parking-lot “price table Phase-0 prerequisite” never lands in a phase. P3/P4 cost sections need `llm_pricing` (config or table) in P0 or early P3 **Scope — in**. |
| high | general plan | Findings devil’s advocate: attempt-row growth + O8 purge. Purge today deletes `github_pipeline_runs` (+ cascade). Attempts need FK cascade from `pipeline_run_id` and/or purge extension — **no phase owns this**. |
